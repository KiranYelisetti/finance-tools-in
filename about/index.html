About QuickTools India

Free, privacy-friendly financial calculators and everyday tools, built for Indian users

Our Mission

QuickTools India was founded with a clear mission: to democratize financial planning for every Indian, whether you're a first-generation investor in a small town or a seasoned professional in a metro city. We believe that access to accurate, easy-to-use financial calculators should not require expensive software, sign-ups, or sharing your personal data.

Every tool we build runs 100% in your browser. Your numbers stay on your device. We don't ask for your phone number, email, or any personal information. We simply want to help Indians make better financial decisions, quickly and privately.

What Makes Us Different

Most online calculators give you a basic EMI result and stop there. We go deeper, because real financial decisions require real context:

Home Loan Calculator

Shows pre-payment impact (how ₹1 Lakh extra/year saves ₹15-20 Lakhs in interest), tax benefits under section 22 (was 24(b)) and section 123 (was 80C), and bank rate comparisons, features that generic calculators skip entirely.

Car Loan Calculator

Goes beyond EMI to show complete on-road price including RTO tax, insurance, and accessories. Compares new vs used car economics so you don't end up "underwater" on your loan.

Investment Calculators

SIP, PPF, SSY calculators with inflation adjustment, step-up SIP projections, and year-wise growth tables, helping you visualize long-term wealth creation.

Tax & Salary Tools

Income Tax Calculator comparing Old vs New Regime, CTC-to-In-Hand Salary Calculator, and GST Calculator with CGST/SGST breakdown, all updated for FY 2026-27 (see the Site Updates page for the dated change log).

How Our Calculators Work

Each calculator uses publicly documented formulas. Here is how we approach them:

1

Public Formulas

EMI uses the standard reducing-balance formula. PPF, SSY, NPS, and tax calculations follow rules published by the Income Tax Department, the RBI, and the relevant scheme notifications. Default interest rates shown reflect publicly listed rates at the time we last updated the page, and may not match your bank or the current government rate.

2

Spot Checks

We compare output against bank EMI calculators and manual spreadsheet computations during development. If you spot a discrepancy, please email us so we can investigate.

3

Estimates, Not Advice

Results are estimates for planning and learning. Before acting on a number, please consult a SEBI-registered investment advisor, chartered accountant, or your bank. We are not financial advisors.

Our Values

Privacy First

All calculations happen in your browser. We don't collect, store, or transmit your financial data. No registration, no phone number, no email, ever.

Always Free

No hidden fees, no premium tiers, no feature-gating. Every tool is 100% free to use, unlimited times.

Education-First Approach

Every calculator page includes detailed explanations, FAQs, and real-world examples. We don't just give you a number, we help you understand what it means for your financial life.

Made for India

Rupee formatting (Lakhs/Crores), Indian tax slabs, GST rates, government scheme rules, everything is tailored for the Indian context. No generic international calculators here.

About This Site

QuickTools India is a small, independent project. There is no large editorial team behind it — just a handful of calculators and explainer articles built carefully and offered free of cost. We are not financial advisors, not affiliated with any bank, mutual fund house, insurance company, or government body, and we do not earn commissions on any product mentioned in our content.

Articles on this site are educational. We try to keep the numbers accurate and the language plain, but tax laws and interest rates change, and we may not update every page immediately. Before making a financial decision, please verify the latest rules with your bank, a chartered accountant, or a SEBI-registered investment advisor.
