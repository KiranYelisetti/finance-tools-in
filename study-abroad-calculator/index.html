Financial Checklist for Parents

  • Proof of Funds: Show liquid funds (FD, Savings) for 1st year expenses.
  • GIC (Canada): CAD 20,635 mandatory deposit for living expenses.
  • Blocked Account (Germany): €11,208 mandatory deposit.
  • Health Insurance: Mandatory for all students (approx $500-$1000/year).
  • Forex Card: Load with foreign currency to save on exchange markup.
  • Scholarships: Apply 9-12 months in advance (they close early).

Sending Your Child Abroad? Here's What You Must Know

Many parents dream of sending their children to study in America, UK, Canada, or Australia. But most parents only look at today's cost and think "I'll save ₹2 crore". This is a BIG mistake! By the time your child is ready to go, the actual cost will be MUCH higher because of two reasons:

Real Example That Shocks Parents:

A USA degree costs ₹1.6 crore TODAY. But if your child is 8 years old now, you'll need ₹4.8 to ₹5.2 crores when they turn 18! That's 3X more money! Why? Because college fees go up every year AND rupee becomes weaker every year.

The Two Hidden Problems:

📈

Problem 1: College Fees Keep Rising

Foreign university fees go up 5-7% every year. In 10 years, a $50,000/year college becomes $100,000/year!

💸

Problem 2: Rupee Keeps Getting Weaker

20 years ago, $1 = ₹45. On 6 Nov 2026 a bank charged ₹94.45 to send $1 abroad. Tomorrow? Maybe ₹120! You need MORE rupees to buy same dollars.
