3. Shape, depth and space

Radius

8, 12, 20 px and pill. Fields use 12, cards 20.

Hard shadow

2, 4, 7 px offsets, no blur. Pressing a button moves it into its shadow.

Space

A 4 px grid: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48. Card padding is fluid, 18 to 30 px.
