    How gratuity is calculated

    Gratuity pays 15 days of wages for every year you worked. Monthly wages are divided by 26, because the law treats a month as 26 working days, so the formula is:

    Gratuity = monthly wages last drawn × 15 ÷ 26 × years of service

    Years of service means completed years, plus one more if the part left over is more than six months. Someone with 7 years and 7 months is paid for 8 years. Someone with 8 years and exactly 6 months is paid for 8.

    The total stops at ₹20 lakh. That is the legal ceiling, and an employer can still pay more under an award, agreement or contract.

    What changed on 21 November 2025

    The gratuity chapter of the Code on Social Security, 2020 came into force on this date and replaced the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972. The formula stayed the same. What changed is who qualifies sooner and what counts as wages.

    The 50% wage rule, with an example

    Many pay structures keep basic low and put most of the salary into allowances. That used to shrink gratuity. Under the Code, if your other pay is more than half of your total pay, the part above half is added back to your wages.

    Take basic plus DA of ₹20,000 and other pay of ₹40,000. Total pay is ₹60,000, so half is ₹30,000. Other pay is ₹10,000 above that line, so wages for gratuity become ₹30,000 and not ₹20,000.

    The Ministry of Labour says performance incentives, ESOPs and reimbursements are not part of wages. If your other pay is half of the total or less, nothing is added and wages stay at basic plus DA.

    Who gets gratuity

    • Permanent employees: after 5 years of continuous service, when they resign, retire or the job ends.
    • Working journalists: after 3 years.
    • Fixed-term employees hired directly: after 1 year under the contract. A further part of more than six months counts as one more year.
    • Death or disablement: no minimum service. On death the money goes to the nominee, or to the heirs if there is no nominee.
    • Contract labour through a contractor: the Ministry of Labour treats this as different from fixed-term employment. The contractor pays, after five years.

    Sick leave, accidents, approved leave, lay-offs, strikes and lock-outs do not break continuous service.

    Tax on gratuity

    For private sector employees covered by the gratuity law, the tax-free part is the lowest of three figures: the amount you received, 15/26 of your last salary for each year counted, and ₹20 lakh. Salary here means basic plus DA, so money added by the 50% rule can be taxable even though your employer must pay it.

    The tax law has not caught up fully. The Income-tax Act, 2025 still names the repealed 1972 Act in this entry, and we found no official note on how it maps to the Code. This page assumes the same limit carries over. Check with a tax professional if a large amount is involved.

    If your employer is not covered by the gratuity law and pays gratuity anyway, a different limit applies: half a month's average salary for each completed year, within a ₹20 lakh lifetime limit. This calculator does not work out that case.

    Where the answer can differ

    • Close to five years: some High Court rulings under the old Act treated 4 years and 240 days as five years. The Code does not say this, so this calculator asks for five full years.
    • Service before the Code: some articles split your career at 21 November 2025. The Ministry of Labour's FAQ of 16 March 2026 instead says gratuity is paid on the wages last drawn under the Code.
    • Fixed-term part years: the Central Rules round up a further part of more than six months. We found no official example for shorter parts, so they are left out here.
    • State rules: this page follows the Code and the Central Rules. Your State's rules may word some details differently.
    • Your contract: an employer can offer better terms than the law, and many gratuity trusts have their own rules.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How is gratuity calculated in India?

    Gratuity is monthly wages last drawn, multiplied by 15, divided by 26, multiplied by your years of service. A part year of more than six months counts as a full year, and exactly six months does not. The amount is capped at ₹20 lakh unless your employer agrees to pay more.

    Can I get gratuity before five years?

    Permanent employees need five years of continuous service and working journalists need three. The minimum does not apply on death or disablement. Fixed-term employees qualify after one year of service under the contract.

    What is the 50% wage rule for gratuity?

    Wages under the Code are basic pay, dearness allowance and retaining allowance. If your other pay is more than half of your total pay, the part above half is added back to wages. In practice, wages become the higher of basic plus DA and half of total pay.

    Does the new rule apply to service before 21 November 2025?

    If you leave on or after 21 November 2025, the Ministry of Labour says gratuity is paid on the wages last drawn, under the Code. The Code also counts service given before it began. If you left before that date, the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 applies.

    What is the maximum gratuity?

    The ceiling is ₹20 lakh. Your employer can pay more under an award, agreement or contract, because the Code protects better terms.

    Is gratuity taxable?

    For private sector employees covered by the gratuity law, the tax-free part is the lowest of the amount received, 15/26 of last salary (basic plus DA) for each year counted, and ₹20 lakh. Anything above that is taxed as salary. Gratuity under government service rules is fully exempt.

    Does a small company have to pay gratuity?

    The chapter covers factories, mines, oilfields, plantations, ports and railway companies, and shops or establishments with 10 or more employees on any day of the previous 12 months. Once covered, an employer stays covered even if staff numbers fall below 10.

    Can government employees use this calculator?

    No. Government employees under their own gratuity rules are outside this part of the Code, so this page does not work out their amount. Central civil employees should check the CCS rules, where the ceiling has been ₹25 lakh since 1 January 2024.
