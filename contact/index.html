Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe when I use your calculators?

Absolutely. All calculations happen 100% in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your financial data. Your numbers never leave your device.

Do I need to create an account?

No. All tools on QuickTools Plus are completely free and require no registration, email, or phone number.

I found an error in one of your calculators. What should I do?

Please email us immediately at quicktoolsindiaa@gmail.com with details of the error, your input values, and the expected result. We take accuracy very seriously and will investigate promptly.

Can I use your tools on my mobile phone?

Yes! All our tools are fully responsive and work perfectly on smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers. No app download required.
