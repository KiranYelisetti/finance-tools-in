Rules change. These pages keep up.

Tax slabs, GST rates and small-savings interest rates change every year or every quarter, so a calculator is only as good as its last check. When a rule changes, the page that uses it is updated and the change is written down on the updates page, with the date.

Where a page depends on a rule, it links to the body that sets it: the Income Tax Department, the GST Council, the Reserve Bank of India or the Ministry of Finance for small-savings rates. The figures here are for planning. Before you sign a loan or file a return, confirm the numbers with the lender or a tax professional.
