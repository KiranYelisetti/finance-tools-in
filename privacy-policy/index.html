Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 4, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to QuickTools Plus ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website at www.quicktoolsplus.com.

By using our website, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Data

We do not collect any personal data. Our calculators and tools operate entirely in your browser. None of your inputs, calculations, or results are transmitted to our servers or stored by us.

2.2 Usage Data

We may collect anonymous usage data through third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use our website. This may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages you visit on our website
  • Time and date of your visit
  • Time spent on pages
  • Device and operating system information

2.3 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our website and hold certain information. Cookies are files with small amount of data that are stored on your device. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. You can also stop Google Analytics from counting your visits with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for various purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our service
  • To analyze how our website is used
  • To improve user experience
  • To detect and prevent technical issues
  • To gather analysis or valuable information to improve our service

4. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. These services include:

  • Google AdSense: For displaying advertisements on our website. Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Google Analytics: For anonymous website traffic analysis to help us understand which tools are most useful and improve our service
  • Font Awesome CDN: For displaying icons throughout our website

These third-party service providers have their own privacy policies addressing how they use such information.

5. Data Security

All calculations and image processing on our tools (SIP Calculator, Home Loan Calculator, Car Loan Calculator, PPF Calculator, SSY Calculator, GST Calculator, Wedding Cost Calculator, Study Abroad Calculator, Income Tax Calculator, Salary Calculator, EMI Prepayment Calculator, Age Calculator, Cash Counter, Number to Words Converter, Image Resizer) are performed entirely in your browser using JavaScript. No data is uploaded to our servers. Your privacy is protected by design.

However, remember that no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted to our website.

6. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and receive a copy of your personal data (though we don't collect any)
  • Opt-out of cookies through your browser settings
  • Request deletion of any data we may have collected
  • Object to processing of your personal data

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is available to all ages. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy.
